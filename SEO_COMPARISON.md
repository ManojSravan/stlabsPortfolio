## Quick Reference: Title Structure

Your new title follows Google's recommended structure:

```
[BENEFIT/IMPACT] [SERVICE] | [BRAND NAME]

"Premium E-commerce Storefronts & High-Converting Landing Pages | Sravan Tech Labs"
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  └────────────────┘
                    VALUE PROPOSITION                           BRAND RECOGNITION
```

This structure:
- ✅ Captures attention (Impact + benefit words first)
- ✅ Includes primary keywords
- ✅ Adds brand at the end for recognition
- ✅ Fits perfectly in search results (55-60 characters)
